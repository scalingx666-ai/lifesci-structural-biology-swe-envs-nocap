## Bug
On step 2, "Start from an example", the popover stopped partway, above the Recent projects section. It only jumped to **Use as project** when the button was hovered.

## Cause
driver.js places the popover halfway through its highlight animation and ignores `refresh()` until the animation ends. Home loads the continue card and recent projects during that window, which pushes the examples down. The tour's layout-change check fired during the animation, where it did nothing, and then recorded the new position as already handled.

## Fix
`TourController` skips the check while driver.js is animating. Once the animation is done it re-places the popover whenever the target's box changes. If the shift pushed the target off screen, it scrolls it back into view once.

## Testing
- `e2e/tour.spec.ts` now inserts a 260px spacer above the examples right after Start, then checks that the popover sits under the button. The test fails without this fix and passes with it.
